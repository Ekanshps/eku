Pricing

Website cost in India in 2026.

The real answer depends on scope, content, design, and business depth. A clearer website often costs less than a bloated one.

Typical estimates vary by project scope and update needs.

A realistic range

Simple business website

₹10,000–₹50,000 for a clean brochure site or a small landing page with clear content and contact flow.

More custom work

₹50,000–₹2,00,000+ when the project includes custom UI, more pages, SEO structure, and ongoing updates.

What changes the price

  1. 01

    Scope

    One-page service sites usually cost less than multi-page business websites with blog and lead capture.

  2. 02

    Content depth

    If text, images and FAQs are already ready, the build moves faster and costs less.

  3. 03

    Ongoing upkeep

    Maintenance, hosting, updates and security are usually separate from the original build cost.
