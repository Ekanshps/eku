Frequently asked questions

For most local businesses, the best website is simple, clear, and built around trust. It should explain what the business does, where it serves, what people can expect, and how to contact or book quickly without friction.

There is no single fixed price, because scope and complexity vary. A small business site can be lean and effective, while a larger build with multiple sections, SEO work, or chat features will cost more. The right approach is to focus on the business outcome instead of adding extras just for appearance.

The answer depends on the business. If the goal is a clear online presence and lead generation, a focused, structured build is often the better option. A custom setup makes sense when there are workflows, complex integrations, or repeated product or service logic to manage.
